How to play

Each round has a set of words, shown a few at a time. Pick one on the left, then its match on the right. A word you answer is replaced by the next one from the round, and the choices reshuffle. A word you miss comes back later in the same round, so the round ends only when you have matched every word. Keyboard: number keys pick on the left, letter keys pick on the right.
